---
title: "How do I launch Atlassify at login?"
category: "Getting Started"
order: 4
---

Atlassify can start automatically when you log in to your computer.

1. Open **Settings** from the Atlassify sidebar
2. Go to the **System** section
3. Toggle **Open at startup**

On Linux, startup behaviour is managed by your desktop environment, so this option may not be available in the app.
