---
title: "How do filters work?"
category: "Using Atlassify"
order: 1
---

Filters help you manage which notifications are shown in the app, so you can focus on what matters most for your workflow.

Filters can be accessed from the Atlassify sidebar. By default, all notifications are shown. You can filter by:

- **Engagement** — comments, mentions, reactions
- **Category** — direct notifications or watching notifications
- **Actors** — notifications from automation, Rovo, or users
- **Read state** — read or unread
- **Products** — notifications for a specific product (e.g. Jira)

If you apply multiple filters, your inbox only shows notifications that match **all** of them (`LOGICAL AND`). For example, selecting **Mentions** and **Unread** shows only unread mention notifications.
