---
title: "How do I control sounds and system notifications?"
category: "Using Atlassify"
order: 3
---

You can control how Atlassify alerts you to new notifications from **Settings → System**:

- **Show system notifications** — display native operating system notifications for new unread notifications
- **Play sound for new notifications** — play a sound when new notifications arrive
- **Volume** — use the volume buttons to adjust the notification sound level (or reset it)

Sounds are also played for the tray/menu bar and other app interactions, all managed from the same System settings.
