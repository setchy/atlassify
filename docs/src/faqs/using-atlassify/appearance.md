---
title: "How do I change the theme and appearance?"
category: "Using Atlassify"
order: 5
---

You can customize the look of Atlassify from **Settings → Appearance**:

- **Theme** — choose **System** (follows your operating system), **Light**, or **Dark**
- **Zoom** — zoom in, zoom out, or reset zoom for the whole app
- **Show account header** — toggle the account header in the sidebar

You can also change the app's **Language** from the Appearance settings.
